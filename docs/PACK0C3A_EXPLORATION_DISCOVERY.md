## State owners and lifecycle

- **Evidence writer:** per-ground/resource occupancy posteriors, qualified coverage, exact discovered-site membership, personal frontier records, contextual/Beta attempt summaries, cumulative paid spend and original-provenance deduplication. A stable personal attempt token survives forgetting a place; reobserving it does not replenish its precedent or occupancy count. The token confers no forgotten location or executable backing. Success records trial-revealed properties and installs personal affordance/method evidence. Failure updates only its cell and the declared broader cell.
- **Mind:** persisted fair trial cursor and existing method/target/comparison cursors; bounded admitted/deferred options and consulted dependencies. New relevant perceived properties can reopen a failed trial; new personal coverage creates new frontier questions. Unchanged zero-information results are suppressed while their contextual precedent remains relevant. Exhausted cumulative spend requires a genuinely changed personal premise, not another label or reload.
- **Body law:** exploration family time/satiation, experienced descriptor familiarity and enjoyment. An inquiry's travel and survey share its activity descriptor; partial settlement does not mint fresh familiarity exposures. Frustration comes from the personal contextual record. No curiosity trait, novelty points or discovery bonus exists.
- **World/material law:** true material kind, effect installation and actual input/output. Hidden matching is indexed at execution completion. Provisional ordinary use applies the declared `0.7 + 0.3κ` factor; repeated observed success refines confidence. A failed match pays the same required Work prefix and produces a real null result.
- **Runtime/goods:** existing paid prefixes, sealed budget, progress, task/retry identity and consumption/input/production transactions. Travel, partial work and survey spend enter the semantic attempt summary. A trial does not silently turn into exploitation; ordinary use receives a fresh authorization.

Schema **8**, model **ESS-4.0-pack0c3a**, rejects incompatible earlier prerelease checkpoints. All causal summaries, cursors, body family state, repertoire records, held draws, spend, prepared route geography pins and pending wakes are persisted. Derived trees/maps rebuild on restore. Exact site/provenance backing and historical audit remain resident; this PR does not claim bounded total memory or provide a new archive service.

## Information boundary and authorization

The mind imports personal evidence and declared public compatibility/physical estimate adapters. It does not import the material-effects catalogue, world sites, truth terrain, true capabilities or resource registries. A hypothetical useful site is a distributional service alternative with no entity, target ID, reservation or material backing. Arrival and paid perception write evidence; renderer frames and decision-panel reads do nothing causal.

Hidden schema matching cannot affect nomination, feasibility, route construction, forecast or first-trial failure timing. The world applies the law after paid execution, then the actor receives the observable outcome through the evidence writer. An unobserving person receives no method. Installing an unused schema in global content updates no personal repertoire.

Optional experimental prefixes obey 0.25 SD time / 1 quiet-food SD and the 0.5 quiet-food-SD reserve from existing accessible backing. The ceiling also applies when an inquiry is composed after another ordinary end. Actual physiology, travel, material use and forgone activities remain in the ordinary forecast. An explicitly paid ordinary upkeep suffix has its own assessed schedule; hoped-for discovery funds none of it.

The existing agenda can represent bounded serial alternatives from ends already admitted in the same review: at most two additional short combinations, including explicitly paid upkeep. This avoids valuing a short trial as if the rest of its slice had no available discretionary activity. These combinations spend descriptor work, use the ordinary rotating comparison admission, and receive the same feasibility/risk/error treatment. They grant no protected exploration action or comparison slot.

## Explicit deferrals and limits

0C3B or later: capital/service investment valuation; tools as investment; generic crafting/production projects; strategic persistence and long prerequisite chains; aspirations and full completion tails; shared assets; teaching, imitation, reports beyond existing channels and social transmission; proposals/institutions; animals, predators and safety; social trial forms; autonomous repair. No new danger/scouting consumer or free process reward for dangerous scouting is introduced; exposure/safety laws remain unimplemented. Inquiry currently concerns the culturally known food-extraction service, and its bounded stock classes/interval are declared approximations. Canonical strike currently has zero decision-relevant instrumental service; its voluntary process value is real, and no eventual tool usefulness is presumed.

Resident audit/provenance growth, mature dense-world performance, full founder generation and the remaining Stage-I/Pack-0 adoption proofs remain open under the existing computational-discipline receipt. No Revision-4 falsifier fired: this slice required no controller, separate arbiter/budget, seventh operation family, hidden target/schema query, guaranteed discovery, unbounded search or exploration bonus/quota.

Reproduce: `npm run check`; `npm run test:browser`; `node --import tsx scripts/exploration-growth.ts`; `node --import tsx scripts/exploration-probe.ts` (one bounded 8/32 panel; do not repeatedly benchmark unchanged code). Existing 0C2 fixtures/tests remain regression consumers.
